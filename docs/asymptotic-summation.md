# Asymptotic summation

The maintained summation guide is [Asymptotic sums](asymptotic-sums.md). It
covers creative telescoping and `asymptotic_rsolve()` handoff, certified Mellin
contour shifts, Gaussian Poisson summation, finite oscillatory sums,
multidimensional sums, rigorous termwise-uniformity certificates,
Euler--Maclaurin, Riemann scaling, and lattice saddles.
